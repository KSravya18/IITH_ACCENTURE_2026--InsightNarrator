Folio 01 — No AI used

Evidence Reconciliation

Sales (weekly), marketing spend (weekly), and customer complaints (irregular timestamps) are merged onto one shared weekly calendar per region before anything is analyzed.
Folio 02 — No AI used

Anomaly Detection Across 5 KPIs

Each KPI is checked against its own rolling statistical baseline. A KPI is only flagged when it deviates enough to matter — both statistically and in real dollar/volume terms.
Folio 03 — No AI used

Investigation Findings

Every flagged revenue anomaly: a price/volume breakdown, cross-referenced marketing and complaint evidence, and a confidence stamp. Click a case to inspect it below.
Folio 04 — No AI used

Clearance Levels

Pick a case above, then view it under three different clearance levels. Restricted fields are redacted rather than hidden. For the Regional Manager, try changing the scoped region to see access get denied live.

Folio 05 — LLM used here (narration only)

Field Report Narratives

The only step where an LLM would be used. It receives the already-verified facts above and is only asked to phrase them for the audience — it cannot introduce a new number, driver, or confidence level. (Shown here via the same offline template the Python backend falls back to without an API key.)
Folio 06 — No AI used

Recommended Actions

Each confirmed driver maps to a specific, owned action with a way to verify it worked. Unconfirmed cases are routed to manual review instead of a guessed action.
